LETTER LEAGUE - BALANCED US ENGLISH WORD LIST

The game's accepted-word list is a curated subset of the project's prior US spelling
lexicon, filtered using the Open American National Corpus (OANC) written word-frequency
CSV supplied for this project. The selection includes alphabetic entries of 3-20 letters
with at least three corpus occurrences, and excludes known British-only spelling variants
and selected foreign-language-only/low-recognition entries. The OANC spoken frequency
CSV was used as a cross-check.

The OANC word-frequency data are derived from the Open American National Corpus.
The corpus and its derived resources are made available under the terms stated by
the OANC resource provider. Consult the original OANC resource page and included
resource documentation for the applicable license and attribution requirements.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION:
Frequency filtering is not the same as expert lexicographic review. This list is a
balanced game vocabulary, not a complete dictionary. Corpus frequency alone cannot
guarantee that every proper name, acronym, archaic term, or foreign-origin term has
been identified perfectly. The list should be reviewed and refined using player
feedback. Both frontend and Supabase use the same generated word list.
